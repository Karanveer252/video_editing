# Shoot guide for the client (phone footage)

Give this to the restaurant or business before they film. Source: BEF cafe and restaurant Reels notes
(research/EXTRACTION.md section 9). Practitioner advice only, none of it measured. UNTESTED for our ads.

## Phone settings
- Shoot vertically (9:16). 1080p or 4K, 30 fps is best. 24 or 60 fps is fine (we convert by optical flow).
- Turn HDR video off if you can (we tone-map it, but SDR keeps the colour truer).
- Tap to lock focus and exposure before you start, so the picture does not pump.
- Clean the lens.

## Light
- Soft side light from a window. No overhead tube lights, no mixing daylight and warm bulbs in one shot.

## What to film
- Open on movement: a pour, a cut, a tear, steam, milk going into a cup. Nothing static for the first shot.
- Let every shot run 2 to 3 seconds longer at the start and end, so we have room to cut on the beat.
- Keep the bottom quarter and right edge of the frame clear of the important stuff (Instagram and Facebook
  put buttons there). Keep the action near the centre.
- Record the sound of the espresso machine, the pan, the knife on its own for 10 seconds. We mix it under the music.
- Screen recordings of your site: record at full brightness, notifications off, scroll slowly, pause 2 s on each section.

## Keep out of shot
- Prices, phone numbers, staff names on badges, receipts, customer faces without their OK.
- Your logo and business name are fine to film, but our ads are brand neutral, so we may crop around them.

## Sending it
- Send the original files (AirDrop, Google Drive, WeTransfer). Not through WhatsApp or Messenger, they shrink the video.
